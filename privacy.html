Privacy Policy

Audio Report AI for Android

Effective date: 5 November 2026 · Last updated: 5 November 2026

1. Scope

This policy describes how the Audio Report AI Android application (“the app”) handles information. Audio Report AI is an independent local-first documentation aid. It is not a medical device and does not provide diagnosis or treatment advice.

2. Information processed on your device

The app processes the following information locally on your device:

  • audio you record with the microphone;
  • audio files you import through the Android system file picker;
  • transcripts, including original and corrected text, timestamps, and review status;
  • speaker names and roles that you assign;
  • report drafts, versions, evidence links, approval status, and writing modes;
  • model files that you import or that are bundled with the app;
  • app settings needed to operate the app.

This information is stored in the app's private storage. Other apps cannot access it through normal Android storage access.

3. No collection or transmission by the developer

The app has no INTERNET permission. It contains no account system, analytics, telemetry, advertising SDK, remote logging, crash-reporting SDK, or automatic upload feature. The developer does not receive your audio, transcripts, reports, speaker labels, modes, or model files.

This website is a static page. It does not include cookies, analytics, or advertising. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may process standard web request logs under GitHub's own policies.

4. Permissions

  • Microphone: used only when you start a recording.
  • Notifications: used to show the recording foreground-service notification and its stop action.
  • Foreground service / microphone service: keeps a user-initiated recording active while the app is in the background and shows a visible notification.
  • Wake lock: helps complete a recording without the device sleeping.
  • File access: the app uses the Android system file picker (SAF) for audio and model import, and for export. It does not request broad storage access.

You can deny or revoke permissions in Android settings. Recording will not work without microphone permission.

5. User-initiated exports

You can export a transcript or report to a destination you choose through the Android system file picker. Once you choose a destination, the app writes the selected file there. The destination app or service may have its own privacy practices; this policy does not cover them.

6. Retention and deletion

Data remains in app-private storage until you delete it. You can delete a transcript, report data, audio, or an entire session inside the app. Uninstalling the app removes its private data, subject to Android backup settings. The app disables Android backup for its data.

There is no developer server copy, so there is no account deletion request to send to the developer. The developer cannot delete data remotely.

7. Security

The app relies on the Android application sandbox and app-private storage. No method of storage is completely secure. Protect your device with a screen lock and keep exported files in a trusted location.

8. Children

The app is intended for adults and professionals. It is not directed to children under 18, and the developer does not knowingly process children's data.

9. Medical and professional disclaimer

Audio Report AI is a documentation aid. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any condition and is not a substitute for professional judgement. Generated reports are drafts and must be reviewed, corrected, and explicitly approved by a qualified person before use. Always verify transcript evidence and clinical content.

10. Models and third-party components

The app can include or use the LFM3 model (LFM Open License v1.0), the OpenAI Whisper model (MIT), llama.cpp (MIT), whisper.cpp (MIT), and AndroidX/Jetpack/Kotlin components (Apache 2.0 and applicable notices). License texts and attribution are included in the app and in the public download repository.

11. Changes

This policy may be updated when the app changes. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.

12. Contact

For privacy questions or support, open an issue at:

https://github.com/isasio/audio-report-ai-android/issues

Never attach audio, transcripts, reports, patient names, or other clinical data to an issue. Use synthetic examples and describe the problem without content.
